Cookie Policy — Consent & Preferences

1. What cookies are and how we use them

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser when you visit a website. They help remember preferences, keep essential features working, and—when you allow it—measure performance and campaign effectiveness.

At 2luv, we use cookies mainly to: (a) remember your consent, (b) provide features and security on some pages (e.g., password-protected content), and (c) when authorized, measure usage and conversions.

2. Types of cookies used and purpose

Necessary: essential for security and basic functionality (e.g., remembering consent and authenticating protected content).

Functional: improve your experience and help navigation (e.g., remembering the last accessed content).

Preferences: store choices like language.

Analytics: help measure and understand site usage (statistics), when enabled.

Marketing: help measure campaigns and conversions, when enabled.

3. How we collect your consent and how long it is stored

When you access 2luv, we show a cookie banner so you can accept, reject, or customize your preferences (e.g., Analytics and Marketing).

Your choice is stored so we can respect it on future visits. In general, we keep the record for up to 12 months (it may vary depending on browser settings). In some cases, we also use the browser's localStorage to store preferences.

4. How you can refuse cookies

You can refuse optional cookies directly in the banner by clicking Reject or by disabling categories in Customize.

You can also manage cookies in your browser settings (block, delete, or limit). If you delete cookies, some preferences may be reset.

6. How to exercise your rights

You can request information and exercise your privacy rights (for example: confirmation of processing, access, correction, anonymization, portability, deletion, and withdrawal of consent), when applicable, by contacting us.

Contact channel: contact@2-luv.com.

7. Changes and modification date of the Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy to reflect changes in our services, technologies, or legal obligations. When there are relevant changes, we will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.

Our story

Almost 40 years of solid-wood craft

A family workshop in White River that has quietly built some of the Lowveld's finest kitchens and cabinetry.

Who we are

Made by hand, made to last

Mi-Hi Solid Wood Products renders luxury and exclusiveness to every home. For close to four decades we have specialised in designer kitchens, built-in cupboards, bars, TV units, bathroom vanities, office furniture and granite & quartz tops.

We work in solid wood, semi-solid wood, Melawood and Duco paint — choosing the right material for each room rather than forcing one answer onto every job. Everything is measured on site, built in our own workshop and installed by our own people.

  • Owner-run and locally accountable in White River
  • Bespoke design — never flat-pack
  • One team from first measure to final handover
  • Repairable, built to be lived in for decades

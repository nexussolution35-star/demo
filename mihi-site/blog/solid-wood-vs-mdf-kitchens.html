Solid Wood vs. Melamine & MDF: Choosing the Right Kitchen Carcass

A plain-language guide to the materials that go into a bespoke kitchen — and where each one earns its place.

Why the carcass matters more than the door

Everyone falls for the door finish first. But the carcass — the boxes behind the doors — decides whether your kitchen still closes squarely in fifteen years. At Mi-Hi we build carcasses to outlive trends, then dress them in whatever finish suits your home.

Solid wood: warmth that ages well

Solid timber brings warmth, repairability and a grain that no print can fake. It moves a little with the seasons, so it wants a maker who understands it. For statement islands, bars and heirloom pieces, nothing else comes close.

"We match material to room, budget and how hard the space works — that's the whole craft."

Melawood & MDF: stable, precise, affordable

Engineered boards stay dimensionally stable and take a Duco or melamine finish beautifully — ideal for handleless, high-gloss and flat-slab looks. Used well, they are not a compromise; they are the right tool for a particular job.

How we decide with you

We match material to room, budget and how hard the space works. A busy family kitchen, a guest bathroom vanity and a display cabinet all deserve different answers — and after almost 40 years, we have built them all.
